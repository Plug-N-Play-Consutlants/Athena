# AthenaEngine v0.7.10.59.3 — Public Player Profile Evidence & Subject Continuity

## Regression repaired

- Plain `Alex Ovechkin` previously returned historical placeholders despite an exact identity map and available official NHL skater summary. The public profile pipeline now recovers a uniquely resolved NHL ID, normalizes L/LW and R/RW, and tries NHL landing directly, validating the returned full name, birth date and position.
- When NHL landing is unavailable, a unique official NHL season-summary observation is displayed with its **actual season label**. It is never represented as current-season production, career totals, or an award inventory. Current stat boxes remain unpopulated until verified target-season evidence exists.
- A long scouting-profile inquiry containing `career legacy` and `evidence limitations` no longer diverts to generational-stature or generic limitations handlers. The resolved player-profile assignment retains precedence.
- Sebastian Aho disambiguation remains an explicit two-way identity decision. The selected Carolina center and Swedish defenseman use separate birth-date/position verification. An older-season NHL index can recover the latter even when absent from current-season summary. A same-name row can never be borrowed from the wrong player.
- Process-lifetime cache of the identity map was replaced by revision-sensitive caching so Sync League can refresh local identity evidence without restarting Scout.

## Evidence boundary

The offline repository currently contains an official 2025-26 NHL skater summary, but no persisted player landing pack. It supports Ovechkin's 2025-26 64 points in 82 games and Carolina Aho's 2025-26 80 points in 79 games, **not** complete career totals. The Swedish Aho's latest NHL season falls outside the recent summary; live older-season NHL acquisition is required for official numbers. Curated biographical/playing-style context is maintained separately from official statistical evidence.

This patch does not alter JHLPAA period scoring, cap rules, or Scout league editorial logic.
