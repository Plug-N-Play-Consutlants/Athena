## Decisions

- Lead once with the verified points leader and the highest-priority closing challenger.
- Include verified gap closed and final deficit, with scoring-window totals only when complete.
- The leading team's weekly scoring breakdown appears once in a subordinate section, rather than repeating the same lead across three headings.
- Use up to two selected competitive contrasts and one independent swing/reversal. Do not re-feature the leader or the headline team.
- Preserve transaction count by week and verified trade asset destinations. Trade participation is context only; do not infer scoring causation or manager intent.
- Avoid `trade(s)` or other machine-generated plural grammar in published prose.
- Conclude with a specific, grounded watch question and one consolidated evidence boundary.
- Partial windows explicitly state that the first requested week's scoring is not verified. No comparative prose is emitted for unverified snapshots.
