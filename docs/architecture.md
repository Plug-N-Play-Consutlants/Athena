# Platform Architecture

The Sports Intelligence Engine is the deterministic core of the Sports Intelligence Platform.

The engine is independent of any provider, sport or product.

## Shared Engine

Fetch
↓
Build
↓
Knowledge
↓
Intelligence
↓
AI

## Product Consumers

- Fantasy Sports Intelligence
- Public Sports Intelligence
- AI Content Platform

Future consumers include APIs and external applications.

## Design Principles

- Provider neutral
- Sport neutral
- Canonical domain model
- Deterministic first
- AI explains deterministic intelligence rather than creating facts
