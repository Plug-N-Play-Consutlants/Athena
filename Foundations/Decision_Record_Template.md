# Decision Record Template

## Decision

Describe the decision being made.

## Context

Explain the problem, constraints, and relevant Athena principles.

## Evidence

List supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty.

## Options Considered

Compare the available solutions, not merely the assets or features.

## Decision Rationale

Explain why this option best supports Athena's North Star.

## Validation

Define how the decision will be tested and what would cause it to be revisited.
