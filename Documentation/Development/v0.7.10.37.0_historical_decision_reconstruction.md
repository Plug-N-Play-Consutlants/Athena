# v0.7.10.37.0 — Historical decision reconstruction

Adds evidence-dated historical decision assessment to the existing bounded cap inquiry execution. It separates pre-decision observations, directly evidenced management statements, observer interpretations, and later outcomes. Every observation must carry a source and publication date; post-decision observations cannot be labeled as contemporaneous. Scout output explicitly distinguishes possible interpretations from known intent and does not infer the decision's quality from outcomes alone.

Input: optional `historical_decision` mapping with `decision`, `decision_date`, `evidence` list (`kind`, `statement`, `source`, `published_date`; `direct_evidence_of_intent` only for a management statement), and `unknowns` list.

## Limitations

This is a **bounded, supplied-evidence capability**. It does not autonomously retrieve historical transactions, confirm a particular real-world trade, reconstruct unrecorded motives, or connect the normal Scout dispatcher. Existing live cap acquisition and full milestone checks remain incomplete. No specific trade claims are hardcoded.
