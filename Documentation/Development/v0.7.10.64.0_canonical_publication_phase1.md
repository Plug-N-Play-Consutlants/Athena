## Proven active route

1. `Athena.entry_policy.select_entry_route` resolves `league_recap`.
2. `Scout.conversation.router.analyze_league` is the compatibility adapter.
3. `Athena.Investigation.period_execution.execute_league_period` selects the
   Fantrax period, retrieves canonical standings/transactions/rosters and audits
   NHL evidence and fantasy scoring reconciliation.
4. `Athena.Investigation.league_recap.research_period` returns
   `athena.league_period_research.v2` plus source-scoped evidence.
5. `Athena.Investigation.editorial_storylines.investigate_storyline_continuity`,
   `editorial_newsroom.historical_trade_continuity`,
   `editorial_player_attribution.editorial_contributors` enrich structured research.
6. `Scout.conversation.role_intelligence.select_scout_role` picks format.
7. `Scout.conversation.journalist_composition.compose_journalist_report` uses
   `Athena.Investigation.editorial_newsroom.rank_stories` for the journalist column.
8. **New single gate:** `compose_editorial_publication` composes and audits
   exactly once. Both journalist and auditor+column routes consume its result.
