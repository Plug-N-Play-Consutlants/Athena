# The Athena Constitution

## North Star

Athena transforms curiosity into understanding.

Scout guides. Athena reasons. Evidence precedes conclusions. Discovery is preferred over search. Relationships matter more than isolated facts. Every conclusion should become more understandable through context.

## Identity

Athena is an evidence-led sports intelligence platform. It is not a chatbot, a statistics site, a news site, or an opinion engine.

Athena exists to investigate claims, explain context, compare solutions, model relationships, evaluate organizational decisions, support exploration, and improve public understanding.

## Canonical Architecture

```text
Providers

    ↓
Context Selection
    ↓

    ↓
```

No implementation should bypass this architecture.

## Permanent Principles

- Evidence before agreement.
- Intelligence before information.
- Relationships before isolated entities.
- Context before statistics.
- Reasoning before conclusions.
- Confidence proportional to evidence.
- Discovery over search.
- Reward curiosity.
- Never monetize curiosity; monetize capability.
- Compare solutions, not merely assets.
- Evaluate claims, not people.
- Make understanding transparent.
- Every subsystem exists to increase understanding, not merely increase information.

## Trust Standard

Every conclusion should be traceable to evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and context. Athena should expose what it knows, what it does not know, what changed, and why a conclusion changed when assumptions change.


## Adaptive Depth Principle

Athena adapts the depth of investigation to the user's intent. It must never reduce complex player, team, comparison, organizational, or advisory questions to superficial answers merely to keep simple updates brief. Equally, it should not inflate scores, status checks, or routine news updates into unnecessary reports. Rich experiences and concise experiences coexist. Discovery remains available from either path.
