# AthenaEngine v0.7.10.67.8 — Transaction Event Chronology and Asset Market Intelligence

## Scope

Correct Period 5 baseline leakage in explicit Periods 6–8 reporting. Reconcile trade-event chronology independently of Fantrax roster-effective period labels and rank complete exchanges by asset count for Scout's editorial.

## Event vs roster effectiveness

- Canonical normalized `transaction_id` identifies the complete exchange (not an individual player-movement row).
- `timestamp` is interpreted in the league's America/Toronto timezone. A verified **scoring** calendar maps event dates to editorial reporting periods.
- A roster-period-only calendar cannot establish an approval event's period. Fantrax's `season_week` is retained as a roster-effective period and, where necessary, as explicitly **unverified fallback**, never mislabeled as approval evidence.
- The league commissioner confirmed that the November 18, 2026 14-player exchange between Grabner By the Jussi and No Regretzkys was approved in Period 8, with roster changes applicable to Period 9. A narrowly scoped, attributed commissioner-attestation rule allows correct event-period attribution even without a verifiable scoring calendar.
- In fixture testing, 14 player movements resolve to one event, 7 assets received per team, zero picks in the exchange.

## Asset-market reasoning

- In this exchange, the commissioner identifies scarce tradable draft picks as the contextual reason managers used player assets as trade currency. The claim is attributed to the commissioner; draft-pick inventory and either party's individual motives have **not** independently been proven.
- For unrelated large player-for-player exchanges, Scout may note that pick scarcity could affect asset markets, but must not claim it caused the trade without inventory or manager-intent evidence.
- Trade size is a **significance signal**, not a valuation verdict. Detailed player quality, keeper eligibility, scoring contribution, and asset opportunity cost require separate evidence.
