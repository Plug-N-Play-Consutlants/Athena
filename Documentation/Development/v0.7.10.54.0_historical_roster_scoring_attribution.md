# AthenaEngine v0.7.10.54.0 — Historical Roster & Scoring Attribution

## Scope / install

**Requires v0.7.10.53.0 source installed first.** This ZIP is a consolidated
source overlay, with the fixed `AthenaEngine/` root directory. Extract over the
existing repo source, then **Reload Build → Verify → Sync League → Scout**.
The ZIP does not contain generated personal runtime data or secrets.

## Evidence-driven implementation

1. **Documented Fantrax historical roster endpoint:**
   `general/getTeamRosters?leagueId=<league>&period=N` via the established
   `FantraxClient`. Fantrax documentation says `period` selects a historical
   lineup period. Current repository `Raw/fantrax_player_pool.json` confirms
   response shape `{period, rosters:{team_id:{teamName,rosterItems:[{id,
   status,position,...}]}}}`. The returned period is checked exactly.
2. **Historical period archive:** Source-native team IDs, player IDs,
   `ACTIVE` / `RESERVE` / `INJURED_RESERVE`, and position are normalized to
   `Output/PeriodRosters/<league>/<season>/period_NNN.json`, with acquisition
   timestamp and provenance. Reject partial team coverage, missing status,
   duplicate player identity, mismatched period, and malformed payloads.
3. **Sync acquisition and bounded backfill:** The **optional** `Archive
   historical period rosters` sync step archives up to the last four elapsed
   periods (Weeks 1–3 at the current checkpoint) and records
   `Output/PeriodRosters/sync_manifest.json`. It never causes the core league
   sync to fail because the optional provider capability is unavailable.
   Later weekly syncs maintain the rolling history. Caches expire after six
   hours; corrupted caches are refreshed, not trusted.
4. **Correct player population:** Official NHL game G+A calculation now joins
   to the historical period roster, when verified, rather than only the
   *current* roster. The NHL game-result cache includes a period-roster
   fingerprint, so a previous current-roster cache cannot leak into a
   historical-period credit analysis.
5. **Evidence of lineup eligibility:** Only `ACTIVE` players are eligible for
   the candidate team sum. `RESERVE` and `INJURED_RESERVE` do not contribute;
   recognized player identity does not establish a game played or a nonzero
   score. Crosswalk gaps remain visible.
6. **Fantrax reconciliation:** For each of 14 teams, compare the sum of
   identified historical ACTIVE skater goals plus assists to the provider's
   cumulative-standings period delta. Distinguish `reconciled`,
   `reconciled_with_identity_gaps`, and `discrepancy`. A standings delta, player
   identity, or historical roster alone is not enough to certify individual
   credited points.
7. **Scout presentation:** Team-by-team evidence and leading named historical
   ACTIVE skaters appear within the existing standings/transaction editorial.
   The full 14-team audit is shown, including discrepancies and gaps. Scout
   continues to separate NHL performance from fantasy league credit.

## Deliberate boundaries

- Some past rostered players may lack Fantrax-to-NHL identity mappings; a
  resolved current-roster crosswalk is *not* a guarantee of historical coverage.
- A historical ACTIVE flag may not, by itself, prove an individual game's
  transaction-effective fantasy credit under every league locking rule.
  Numeric reconciliation promotes an exact score match; discrepancies remain
  explicit until Fantrax's credited per-player evidence can resolve them.
- Historical Fantrax standings can be revised. An elapsed calendar window is
  not proof of formal standings finalization.
- Trading blocks remain low-priority advertised marketplace intent, not
  required scoring-period evidence.
