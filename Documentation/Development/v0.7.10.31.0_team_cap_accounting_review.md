# v0.7.10.31.0 Team Cap Accounting Coverage Review

Adds `Reasoning.Cap.team_cap_accounting_review.review_team_cap_ledger` as a deterministic coverage and reconciliation gate for canonical itemized charges. It computes itemized subtotals by obligation class, flags duplicate active subject/class observations, withholds total and headroom until canonical coverage is complete, and refuses to compare published totals with different dates or accounting bases. It produces a concise Scout-safe narrative that does not misstate partial evidence as cap space.

This is not a live contract provider, a calculation of LTIR relief, daily cap accrual, or a complete real-team cap position. The source evidence required to certify those remains outstanding. It does not register a new Studio button.

Validation: `python -B Tests/validate_team_cap_accounting_review_0710310.py`, release hygiene, and existing cap tests. Studio Verify Build and runtime Scout acceptance remain user-run.
