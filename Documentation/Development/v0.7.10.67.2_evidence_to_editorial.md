# v0.7.10.67.2 — Multi-period evidence-to-insight and Scout publication

Baseline: v0.7.10.67.1, including accepted Studio Sync League snapshot.

## Scope and design
- Athena's multi-period analyst retains source-bounded, team-by-team cumulative rank/points comparison and independently calculates week production when the prior-period snapshot is present. The full requested-window total is never confused with a sum of differences after its first snapshot.
- Athena emits structured, source-bound signals: full-window scoring pace, large week-on-week scoring changes, standings reversals, and overall position changes. These are observations, not causal inference about managers, trades, injuries, or lines.
- The transaction path preserves unique completed, season-week attributed events. When canonical trade assets carry verified movement directions, the editorial names both receiving teams and their incoming players. Missing directions remain explicitly unverified rather than a generic fabricated deal description.
- Scout presents a news-led narrative from Athena's structured results. The league multi-period inquiry now selects journalist voice, not an unrelated scouting-assessment heading. Professional consultation for other question types is unchanged.
- The Studio Sync League pipeline and successful operational league snapshot remain unchanged by this overlay.
