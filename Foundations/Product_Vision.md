# Product Vision

Athena's product vision is to transform sports curiosity into evidence-led understanding.

Athena should support public analysis, fantasy intelligence, organizational evaluation, comparative exploration, counterfactual reasoning, and decision intelligence through a shared intelligence architecture.

The experience should feel less like search and more like an investigation: the user becomes curious, Scout helps frame the question, Athena assembles evidence, relationships emerge, understanding grows, and deeper questions naturally appear.


## Multiple Depths, One Athena

Athena serves quick information needs and deep investigations without turning one into the other. A score can be immediate. News can be concise and explorable. Player, team, comparison, organizational, and fantasy questions can remain information-rich when the user's intent calls for understanding rather than retrieval.
