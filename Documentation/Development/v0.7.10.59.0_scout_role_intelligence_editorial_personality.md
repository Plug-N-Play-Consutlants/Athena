## Implemented

1. `Scout.conversation.role_intelligence` selects voice from task semantics, not an arbitrary mode-only personality. Forensic scoring/reconciliation is *auditor*, weekly league reportage *journalist*, player/deployment and improvement evaluation *analyst*, and asset/keeper/cap/trade decisions *executive*. Explicit user voice overrides are allowed except when an audit's integrity requirements apply. Role selection is recorded in `developer.scout_role`.
2. `Scout.conversation.journalist_composition` renders Athena's verified `athena.league_period_research.v2` findings as an actual sports column: news lead, select standings stories, transaction wire, developing picture, quiet corner, independently labelled NHL spotlight, and next-week hook. It limits repeated team callouts, avoids baseless quotations/manager motives, and gives a little humour only to observed facts. The columnist does **not** reassign Fantrax fantasy credit from NHL production.
3. **Role-safe audit presentation:** an explicit reconciliation/diagnostic request continues to use the existing full all-team period audit; an explicit combined audit-plus-editorial request places a separate sports-desk column after the verified audit. Existing numerical evidence and limitations are preserved.
4. `Athena.Investigation.editorial_storylines` produces legitimate two-period rank trajectories **only** when three complete canonical standings snapshots from the same league and season have matching team IDs. Otherwise Scout reports a single verified period movement, not a fabricated streak.
5. Executive/scouting advisory answers are presented through distinct bounded framing while retaining the specialist's conclusion, confidence and unknowns. Weak-evidence gap responses stay gaps. The public visible aliases and regenerated AthenaResponse contract receive the framing together; role metadata is in developer diagnostics.
6. Ordinary recap output sets `editorial_report=True` for the Scout presentation surface. It does not display scoring-debug information unless asked to audit or a substantive scoring exception needs disclosure.

## Acceptance boundaries

Role-aware communication and narrative continuity are accepted only if factual limits remain intact. Rank movement is not automatically a story of manager intent; a trade is not evidence of performance causation; an unrecorded transaction is not a claim that a manager did nothing. This version does not add live game-effective lineup data, individual historical Fantrax point credit, trading block acquisition, or a speculative/LLM narrative generator.
