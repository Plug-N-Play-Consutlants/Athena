## New bounded differential

The tool requests `getLiveScoringStats` for that date and the preceding calendar date (a negative control, **not** an inferred scoring-period boundary), using the independently source-reviewed authenticated Fantrax request contract. It computes in-memory player/team-aligned numeric comparisons, memberships, number of teams with changed numeric aggregate totals and two truncated SHA-256 fingerprints. No raw authenticated responses, names, IDs, individual points, fantasy team IDs, cookies or tokens are persisted/exported. Source responses remain in memory for the duration of the diagnostic only.

`object1` remains a **numeric candidate**. A difference between responses can indicate time-varying source data, but cannot establish that the API respected either requested date. Even a returned date marker may be an echo, not proof of source semantics. For historical credit, still require provider calendar date-to-period mapping, actual lineups at the date, meaning of `object1` and reconciliation of all daily scoring to official completed team-period totals, including late scoring corrections.

In every case `verified_period_credit=false`, `ledger_written=false`, `credit_eligible=false`. No scoring ledger integration or new permanent Studio controls. The existing undated player-period diagnostic remains unchanged.
