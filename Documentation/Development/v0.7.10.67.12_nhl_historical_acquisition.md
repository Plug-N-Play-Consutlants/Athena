## Corrections
- `Athena/Investigation/nhl_period_production.py` now retrieves a full game-granular NHL stats report when NHL Stats REST truncates `limit=-1` relative to the declared `total`. Explicit fixed-width offset pages are re-fetched from zero, with bounded maximum row count and complete coverage verification.

- Studio Operations Console validator and doctor now check the v67.11 Core Workflow evidence-refresh action and retain the repository-review tool in Developer Mode, instead of expecting its old layout.
