# AthenaEngine v0.7.7.3.1 — Analytical Study & Evidence Reconciliation

## Objective
Close the 0.7.7 inquiry-utilization arc by presenting completed investigations as analytical studies rather than execution reports, while preserving unresolved user claims as testable hypotheses instead of either accepting or discarding them.

## Changes
- Adds sport-neutral evidence reconciliation primitives with explicit unresolved-claim preservation.
- Adds an `analytical_study_v1` product to NHL transaction investigation.
- Grounds transaction study in the competitive objective: improve the organization's path to competitive success within governing rules and the relevant horizon.
- Scout transaction composition now presents study dimensions such as Scenario Assessment, Assets on the Table, organizational needs, Proposed Construction, Financial Impact, Rules & Mechanics, and Athena's Verdict.
- Organizational-asset inquiries now respect prospect-specific filtering instead of returning the same all-assets response.
- Adds Studio Verify doctor and validator for analytical-study/evidence-reconciliation contracts.
- No new Studio button.
