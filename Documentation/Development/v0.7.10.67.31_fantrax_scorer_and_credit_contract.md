2. Safe schema counts describe scorer identity coverage without storing player IDs, names or FPts in the diagnostic report.
3. A **read-only**, standalone `ACTIVE.statsMap` structure inspector documents the alternative credited-lineup evidence pathway without granting any credit or making speculative requests.
