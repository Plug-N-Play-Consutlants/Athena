# AI Content Platform

The AI Content Platform is a product built on top of deterministic intelligence generated by the Sports Intelligence Engine.

It does not calculate facts independently. It explains, summarizes and formats intelligence already produced by the pipeline.

## Product Responsibilities

- Articles
- Weekly summaries
- Pick'em content
- Statistical stories
- Fantasy articles
- Team reports
- Player trend reports
- League recaps

## AI Boundary

AI must not invent facts, bypass provider normalization or reason directly over raw provider payloads.
