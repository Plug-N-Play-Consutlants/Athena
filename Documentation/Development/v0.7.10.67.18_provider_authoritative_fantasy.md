# v0.7.10.67.18 — Provider-Authoritative Fantasy Scoring

## Decision
Fantasy reports use the league provider's official fantasy scores. NHL G+A and independent league score reconciliation are optional analytical/diagnostic evidence, **not a prerequisite** for routine fantasy reporting. Scoring rules can differ among leagues. Never calculate fantasy points from NHL G+A unless explicitly doing a rules-based comparison and labeling it as calculated.

## Changes
- Standings and team weekly values continue to come from archived provider standings.
- Multi-period publication no longer runs `analyze_player_attribution` (the independent NHL active-roster sum) or `diagnose_period_evidence` automatically. These remain available to the standalone Studio evidence diagnostic.
- `Athena/Investigation/provider_fantasy_points.py` provides an adapter-neutral, strict `PeriodFantasyPoints` credited-player ledger contract and exact period/league/season/provider loader.
- Scout publishes provider-credited individual points when, and only when, an adapter has supplied verified historical credited-player rows. No NHL-derived fallback or unexplained reconciliation claims appear in routine reports.
- No new Studio buttons.

## Provider ledger adapter contract
A provider adapter can persist `Output/PeriodFantasyPoints/<provider>/<league_id>/<season>/period_NNN.json`, with schema `athena.provider_period_fantasy_points.v1`, `provider`, `league_id`, `season` (string), `period` (integer), `status=provider_credited_period`, `verified=true`, nonempty `source`, and `records` containing `player_id`, `player_name`, `team_id`, `team_name`, `fantasy_points` and `credit_basis=provider_period_credited`.

**Important:** `verified=true` is not a generic network-success signal. The provider adapter must independently establish the requested scoring period and credited-points semantics. Current Fantrax `getPlayerStats` still lacks a confirmed single-period request/response contract; its `BY_PERIOD` season selector alone is insufficient. This release does **not** claim live acquisition of credited player-period fantasy points. Absent these records, Scout still uses official team standings/period totals and refrains from inventing player contributors.

## Future integration
Verify Fantrax's authentic per-period player FPts selector and response metadata, then have its provider adapter generate this normalized archive. Other providers can implement the same ledger without NHL scoring assumptions. For fantasy inquiries routed from Public/Normal mode, reuse the same provider-scoped investigation; independent NHL/fantasy scoring comparisons remain explicit research tasks.
