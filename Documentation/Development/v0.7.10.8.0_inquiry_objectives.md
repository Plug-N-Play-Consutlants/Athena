# v0.7.10.8.0 — Inquiry Objective Ledger

## Scope

Introduces a shared conservative objective-decomposition contract for both Athena request entry points. Original inquiry clauses and independently *unverified* objective requirements are exposed to developers and traces; explicit evidence gaps are associated with each objective for diagnosis. The completion assessor records the question's detected objectives without accepting a specialist's self-declared completion as independent proof.

This is an **observability and planning foundation**, not completed multi-specialist orchestration. No speculative trade recommendations, invented cap figures, or unsupported completion claims are added. Objective classification is lexical and deliberately non-authoritative; multi-capability execution, claim-to-source provenance and answer-coverage verification remain future work.
