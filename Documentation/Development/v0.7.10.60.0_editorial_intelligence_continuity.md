# AthenaEngine 0.7.10.60.0 — Editorial Intelligence and Continuity

## Scope

This is a scoped overlay over **v0.7.10.59.4**. It changes fantasy editorial research, narrative selection, publication audits and their regression tests; it does not change the canonical scoring implementation, Fantrax acquisition or professional player evidence. Professional profile restoration remains an independently open issue.

## Architectural handoff

1. Athena's `research_period` retains the **complete** `league_editorial` transaction evidence, not only a high-significance `trade_cluster` finding.
2. `editorial_newsroom.rank_stories` ranks verified week-specific scoring, movement, repeated trends, and trade clusters without presuming trade motives or scoring causality. A trade count alone is not forced to be the headline.
3. `editorial_newsroom.transaction_inventory` uses every canonical trade event by unique transaction ID, with deterministic deduplication if only finding-level evidence exists. It records source and original expected count.
4. Athena's `historical_trade_continuity` detects a team making canonical trades in **both** adjacent completed periods and reports the combined count. It makes no claim about absent trade evidence.
5. Scout's journalist composer publishes a separate standings/scoring lead, full trade coverage exactly once, verified multi-week arcs and bounded player/team attribution. The separate technical audit retains detailed evidence limitations.
6. `audit_publication` checks count consistency, exact asset coverage, trade section presence and leakage of the diagnostic limits block. The journalistic publishing route **holds** output instead of silently dropping a trade if its audit fails.
7. Cross-month periods use complete month names, e.g., October 26–November 2, 2026.
