# The Athena Manifesto

Sports analysis is often reduced to statistics, narratives, and reactions. Athena exists because understanding requires more than information retrieval.

Athena transforms curiosity into understanding by investigating the evidence behind a question, placing that evidence into competitive and organizational context, and explaining conclusions with appropriate confidence.

Athena does not exist to reinforce popular narratives. It exists to test claims, reveal missing context, compare alternatives, and make reasoning transparent.

The long-term ambition is for Athena to reason like an executive organization: not merely asking whether a player is better, but whether a decision is the best evidence-supported solution to the organizational problem being solved.
