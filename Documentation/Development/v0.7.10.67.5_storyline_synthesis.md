# AthenaEngine v0.7.10.67.5 — Competitive Storyline Synthesis

## Purpose

Build a bounded Athena-owned analytical synthesis layer after multi-period scoring, race, and transaction acquisition, before Scout editorial presentation. Preserve v67.4, v67.3 shutdown and v67.1 Sync League operator fixes.

## Behaviour

- `Athena/Investigation/storyline_synthesis.py`: pure deterministic evidence-ranking across the entire verified team cohort. Scores contextual significance from championship lead, gap closure/loss, verified full-window production, large adjacent-week scoring shifts, standing movements and recorded trade participation.
- `Athena/Investigation/multi_period_analysis.py`: invokes synthesis only after all requested evidence acquisition; stores structured `synthesis` under `developer.multi_period_findings` with all ranked teams and the top 3 featured narratives.
- `Scout/conversation/multi_period_publication.py`: presents the ranked stories under **The competitive storyline**, connecting verified performance and race trajectory in readable prose. The transaction wire remains separate for documented asset movements. One clearly bounded evidence warning replaces repeated explanatory disclaimers.
- Transaction inclusion denotes **same-week co-occurrence**, never that a trade caused performance or that a manager had a particular motive. Player-level causality and future outcomes remain unverified.
- If standings are missing, no fabricated article is generated. If race data are incomplete, editorial does not invent gap changes. If Week 4 baseline is missing, production still uses partial-window labeling.
