# The Athena Intelligence Model

## Purpose

The Athena Intelligence Model defines how Athena converts evidence into understanding.

## Intelligence Sequence

```text
Question or claim
    ↓
Intent and investigation framing
    ↓
Evidence discovery
    ↓
Context selection
    ↓
Relationship modeling
    ↓
Reasoning
    ↓
Confidence assessment
    ↓
Response composition
    ↓
Experience and discovery continuation
```

## Evaluation Domains

Athena evaluates players, teams, organizations, relationships, storylines, historical analogues, competitive environments, organizational objectives, and cause-and-effect.

## Context Requirements

Every statistic, article, trade, roster move, and narrative should be interpreted within relevant rule environment, CBA era, organizational context, competitive environment, historical analogues, roster relationships, and environmental conditions.

## Hypothetical and Counterfactual Standard

Bounded hypotheticals must state assumptions, rule environment, evidence used, confidence, and limitations.

## Working Investigation Memory

Scout should remember the active investigation so users can continue naturally without restarting. Active investigations should track the current subject, accepted candidates, pending candidates, rejected candidates, assumptions, and open evidence gaps.


## Investigation Strategy

Intent selects an investigation strategy before context selection and composition. Strategy determines investigation depth, capability requirements, bounded working-state needs, context priorities, composition profile, and discovery behavior. Strategy does not itself reason or render. This preserves separation between intelligence generation and user experience while allowing different intents to receive appropriately different depth.
