# v0.7.10.67.9 — Historical Player Attribution and Trade Impact Boundaries

## Objective

Connect archived NHL goals+assists by scoring period to **that period's historical Fantrax active roster**, and reconcile with verified cumulative team standings before describing individual production as credited fantasy scoring.

## Evidence and authority

- `Output/PeriodPlayerProduction/<league>/<season>/period_NNN.json`: verified NHL scoring window, Fantrax player ID crosswalk; **not automatically credited fantasy points**.
- `Output/PeriodRosters/<league>/<season>/period_NNN.json`: historical period roster owner, active versus reserve designation.
- `Output/Standings/<league>/<season>/period_NNN.json`: official cumulative fantasy scores; period delta is the difference from period N-1.
- Provider trade events: approval period, roster effective period, asset direction and identifiers must remain independent dimensions.

## Implementation

`Athena/Investigation/player_level_attribution.py` acquires the two historical archives for each requested period. It uses Fantrax IDs, ignores reserve scoring, rejects duplicated player identities, missing/uncertified archives, and malformed numeric observations. Only a complete historical active roster whose NHL scoring aggregate equals official team scoring delta can be labelled `reconciled_active_nhl_points` (reconciliation, not attribution of trade causality). Other teams retain `partial_nhl_evidence_not_fantasy_credit`. Input status and per-team results remain available in `multi_period_findings.player_attribution`. Scout mentions named NHL scorers only in reconciled cases, with caveats elsewhere.

Trade-impact status explicitly blocks backdating production before roster effectiveness. Once a post-effective period exists, actual contribution still requires immutable transaction player ID crosswalks, rather than matching display names or current ownership. This version **does not claim a complete pre/post trade value model**, manager intention or causality.

## Archive acceptance observation

The supplied November 20 base ZIP contains player production and historical rosters for Periods 6 and 7, but not Period 8. On the archived standings, 2 of 14 team totals reconcile in Period 6 and 2 of 14 reconcile in Period 7. This is a diagnostic result; it does **not** prove the other teams' Fantrax scoring is wrong. NHL window boundaries, roster lock, eligibility and league scoring rules may account for discrepancies, and require targeted subsequent investigation. Missing Period 8 data in that archive is not proof that the user's updated local sync lacks it.
