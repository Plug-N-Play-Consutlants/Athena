# v0.7.10.67.4 — Competitive race synthesis

Baseline: v0.7.10.67.3 (Scout lifecycle regression provisionally accepted).

## Objective

Extend Athena's bounded multi-period league investigation beyond ranked metrics to identify actual championship-race implications: leader(s), closest challengers, change in the points gap to first and teams losing ground. Keep Athena responsible for computed findings and Scout for readable reporting.

## Implementation

- Added pure `Athena.Investigation.competitive_race.analyze_race` analysis on verified cumulative standings snapshots. It computes team-relative points gaps from the highest observed end-of-period score, not ranks alone.
- When a preceding period snapshot is verified and identity membership matches, the gap movement covers the full requested set of weeks (e.g. end of Week 4 through end of Week 7 for Weeks 5–7). Otherwise, the comparison begins at the first requested snapshot and the user-visible report labels that limitation.
- Equal points at first are reported as a shared lead rather than inventing a sole leader. Never infer causes, trade success or player scoring attribution.
- The structured race is exposed in developer diagnostics `multi_period_findings.race`; Scout's multi-period journalist composes a concise "The race for first" section from those findings.
- Previous v67.3 process lifecycle changes and v67.1 league-wide Sync League output are included unchanged.

## Verification

- 21 targeted regressions passed across new v67.4 cases, v67.2 editorial, v67.0 period analysis, v67.3 Scout lifecycle, and v67.1 Studio sync.
- Archive smoke test: all 14 JHLPAA teams for weeks 5–7 validated. After Week 7, Leeeroy Jenkins leads with 304; Bubba's Bruisers and Grabner By the Jussi have 280 (24 back). Bubba closed 17 points of the lead gap between end of Week 4 and end of Week 7.
- Additional broader suite: 29 passed, 1 failed due to pre-existing old standings fixture (`Tests/test_scout_role_personality_0710590.py`) assigning a lower-ranked team more points. Do not weaken the rank/points validator. Full repo suite not run; live Windows Studio test pending.
