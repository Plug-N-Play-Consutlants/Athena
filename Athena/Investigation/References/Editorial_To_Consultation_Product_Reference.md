# AthenaEngine — Editorial-to-Consultation Product Reference

Status: Qualitative user-observation reference and future acceptance input; not canonical hockey transaction evidence.
Updated: 2026-10-15
Baseline: v0.7.10.6.0

## Experience pattern
Weekly JHLPAA editorial reports introduce Athena's voice to league members who have not seen the application. Earlier reports received reactions. On October 15 a previously nonparticipating member spontaneously asked "Athena have any answers" in a pool-chat discussion about Toronto acquiring Aatu Räty from Vancouver. This is an early observed transition from editorial exposure to asking for analysis, not evidence of broad adoption.

## Representative inquiry
Why would Toronto trade for a player who had reportedly been available on waivers, and how would the NHL 50-contract limit affect the decision?

## Required reasoning dimensions
- Verify transaction participants, dates and waiver sequence from authoritative/current sources.
- Distinguish waiver eligibility from actual waiver placement, clearance, and claim priority.
- Distinguish standard player contract limits from active NHL roster limits and assignment/waiver rules.
- Assess how a two-for-one transaction affects contract slots, conditional on verified contract counts.
- Explain plausible organizational benefits while explicitly separating rules and observed facts from unverified management intent.
- Do not infer a future defensive acquisition merely because a contract slot opens.
- Communicate a coherent, accurate answer in an 800-character chat format when requested.

## Acceptance benchmarks
1. Unknown NHL transaction question reaches a general investigative path, not a Leafs-specific canned answer.
2. Athena decomposes the question into transaction, waivers, contract capacity, roster assignment, and intent/uncertainty.
3. Every factual claim carries traceable source and temporal context; unsupported premises remain conditional.
4. Athena evaluates alternatives and explains why the transaction could be rational without asserting private motives.
5. Scout can generate both a detailed answer and a faithful <=800-character version.
6. Weekly editorial and conversational answers reuse the same evidence and analytical contracts while differing in presentation.
7. Do not count report reactions or consultation requests as direct app usage.
