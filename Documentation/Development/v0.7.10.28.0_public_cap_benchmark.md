# v0.7.10.28.0 - Public cap benchmark reconciliation

## Source-first finding

Existing `Knowledge/Contracts` acquisition pathways require a publisher to provide canonical itemized contract obligations. NHL roster evidence alone cannot supply contract AAV, LTIR, retention, buyouts, bonuses, or daily cap accounting. CapWages publishes itemized team contract tables but its documented REST API requires a paid subscription. No unlicensed CapWages gateway integration was introduced.

Sportsnet publicly presents *published* projected cap hit, projected cap space, today's space and ceiling on team pages. These are useful independent comparison figures but **are not equivalent to verified itemized accounting**. Different providers and different accounting dates may report legitimately different figures. Sportsnet's page-format sample for Toronto was observed on 2026-10-16; values are not seeded as canonical current state.

## Implemented

- `Knowledge/Contracts/public_cap_benchmark.py`: opt-in single-request Sportsnet published cap benchmark for 32 NHL team pages. Source URL and retrieval time preserved. Parser rejects missing fields, inconsistent ceiling/space arithmetic, unsupported team identities, non-200 responses, redirects, and oversized responses.
- `PublishedCapBenchmark.compare(ledger)`: compare only when team matches; incomplete canonical ledgers return `None` rather than a fabricated discrepancy. No benchmark is converted into contract charges, LTIR relief or verified completeness.
- `Tests/validate_public_cap_benchmark_0710280.py`: six deterministic offline tests for schema drift, identity, opt-in acquisition and no false coverage.

## Provider access boundaries

- **Sportsnet**: public team salary sections are accessible to readers. Automated acquisition must be explicitly authorized after verifying applicable publisher terms and robots policy; no background crawl, login bypass, or scraping schedule is enabled.
- **CapWages**: public team contract tables and paid documented REST API. No automatic collection is implemented pending permission and supported access. Do not guess private endpoints.
- **Fantrax**: fantasy salary fields are not NHL SPC/AAV proof. Keep them separate unless independent contract provenance establishes equivalence.
- **PuckPedia**: request no-cost evaluation or research access; no entitlement assumed.
- **ESPN/theScore**: supplemental news, roster and transaction context, not verified complete salary-cap sources.
