# Public Sports Intelligence

Public Sports Intelligence is a product built on top of the shared Sports Intelligence Engine.

It consumes canonical organization, roster, player, transaction, salary, prospect and competitive-window intelligence to analyze real-world sports questions.

## Product Responsibilities

- Professional team analysis
- Trade scenario analysis
- Salary cap and roster-fit analysis
- Coaching and deployment impact analysis
- Prospect path and blockage analysis
- Organizational direction analysis
- Public-facing team and player reports

The product should reuse the shared engine rather than creating a separate public-sports calculation path.
