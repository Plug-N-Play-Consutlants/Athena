# AthenaEngine v0.7.7.1.1 — Investigation Depth & Completion Hotfix

## Objective
Prevent transaction investigation from declaring saturation after merely discovering candidate assets. Material candidates must be enriched, compared and used in a named construction attempt before the inquiry can be considered complete.

## Behavioral Contract
- Candidate discovery is not investigation saturation.
- Consequential candidate assets are enriched through the existing NHL player-landing capability.
- Draft pedigree, age/development state, current production, position and handedness remain evidence, not hidden market-value scores.
- First-overall/high-draft status is treated as material analytical significance when the provider supplies it; no player is hardcoded.
- Athena compares seller-side fit with buyer-side opportunity cost without inventing front-office intent.
- A transaction inquiry attempts a strongest-current-evidence named package before saturation.
- The package is explicitly an analytical construction, not a claim of seller acceptance or market sufficiency.
- "Build the strongest named package" is no longer offered as a follow-up after Athena has already been asked whether the transaction can be done.
