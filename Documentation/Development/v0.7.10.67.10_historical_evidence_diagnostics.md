# v0.7.10.67.10 — Historical Evidence Diagnostics & Credit Provenance

## Investigation result
The base snapshot has verified period production and historical period rosters for Periods 6 and 7, but not Period 8. Numerical agreement between period ACTIVE-roster NHL goals+assists and official team cumulative standings differences occurs for only a minority of teams. The existing canonical PeriodScoringAudit archives specifically carry `lineup_eligibility: period_snapshot_not_game_effective` and `credited_fantasy_points_verified: false`, including when a numerical sum agrees. Hence *no* individual credited Fantrax scoring attribution can be proven from the current evidence merely through equality.

## Implementation
- Read-only `period_evidence_diagnostics` reports each period's production/roster availability, numeric mismatch count and magnitude, unresolved active identities, and hypotheses not established as causes.
- `player_level_attribution` explicitly distinguishes NHL G+A under a historical period ACTIVE label from proven game-effective credited fantasy points.
- Scout can mention a named player's **observed NHL** production without declaring verified fantasy credit. Its article identifies the coverage gap and team-period reconciliation counts.
- `Tools/diagnose_period_evidence.py` runs read-only by default. With `--refresh` it reuses the existing authenticated `sync_period_scoring_audit.run(requested_periods=...)`, whose period-calendar validation and source-provenance requirements remain intact. It does not make up historical lineups or silently fill gaps from today.

## Windows operations
After installing the cumulative overlay and reloading Studio, run:

```powershell
cd F:\Development\AthenaEngine
C:\Python314\python.exe Tools\diagnose_period_evidence.py --periods 6 7 8
```

To **attempt** recovery after confirming authenticated Fantrax access and calendar boundaries:

```powershell
C:\Python314\python.exe Tools\diagnose_period_evidence.py --periods 6 7 8 --refresh
```

`--refresh` does not guarantee a period is recovered; check the returned `acquisition_manifest.periods` statuses and `after` snapshot. Preserve revised historical evidence and do not take numeric equality as game-effective credit. Verify Build in Scout Studio and rerun the same user question.

## Remaining engineering dependency
Game-by-game Fantrax roster locks / lineup eligibility and credited point ledger, plus confirmed scoring boundary alignment, are still needed to explain why Period 6/7 differences exist and establish actual individual fantasy scoring credit. They cannot be inferred from a period roster label or NHL game summary.
