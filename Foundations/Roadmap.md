# Roadmap

## Current Direction: Epic 6 Experience Layer

Continue building the Player Experience, Team Experience, Organization Experience, Comparison Experience, News Experience, Fantasy Experience, and Evidence Panels.

Enrich dossiers with identity, age, jersey number, salary, cap hit, contract, awards, leadership, international play, development pathway, season-by-season statistics, and historical context.

## Next Major Evolution: Decision Intelligence

Future intelligence domains include Relationship Intelligence, Organizational Optimization, Solution Intelligence, Comparative Intelligence, Counterfactual Intelligence, Environmental Intelligence, Competitive Identity Intelligence, Historical Analogue Intelligence, Market Intelligence, Scenario Intelligence, and Working Investigation Memory.

These domains must be layered onto the existing architecture rather than implemented as isolated features.


## Epic 6 / Sprint 4 — Adaptive Investigation Strategy

Establish intent-aware investigation strategies, bounded working investigation state, context-priority contracts, composition profiles, capability discovery, and diagnostics. Preserve the validated rich player/team/comparison experiences while enabling concise score/news/update experiences with deeper discovery available.

## Future Objective — Sports Ecosystem Investigative Intelligence

Athena must ultimately understand sport as an ecosystem rather than only the events occurring on the field. Future investigations may traverse Sport → League → Franchise → Ownership → Corporate Affiliates → Financing → Revenue → Media Rights → Labor/CBA → Contracts → Debt/Obligations → Regulators → Governance → Markets → Stakeholders.

This is a future architectural/evaluation objective, not a v0.6.4 implementation requirement. It should be built through insertion-adaptive contracts, relationship discovery, shared claim/evidence context, hypothesis/falsification discipline, and discoverable cross-domain capabilities rather than sport-, league-, franchise-, or owner-specific pathways.
